INDIA GATE

The India Gate is a majestic 42-metre-tall war memorial archway located in the heart of New Delhi, India, standing at the eastern end of the ceremonial boulevard known as Kartavya Path. Designed by the prominent British architect Sir Edwin Lutyens and unveiled in 1921, this sandstone structure was built to honor the 70,000 soldiers of the British Indian Army who lost their lives during the First World War and the Third Anglo-Afghan War. The names of thousands of these fallen soldiers are intricately inscribed directly onto the walls of the monument. Beneath the towering arch lies the Amar Jawan Jyoti (Flame of the Immortal Soldier), a glowing eternal flame dedicated to all unknown soldiers who sacrificed their lives for the nation. Surrounded by vast, manicured lawns and fountains, the monument is a popular gathering spot for locals and tourists alike, glowing beautifully at night under colorful floodlights.
